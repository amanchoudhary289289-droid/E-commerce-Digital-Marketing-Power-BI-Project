# E-commerce-Digital-Marketing-Power-BI-Project
**Project Purpose:**

This Power BI dashboard analyzes marketing campaign performance across different channels, audiences, and time periods. The project helps marketing teams understand which campaigns, channels, and audience segments generate the highest revenue and return on ad spend (ROAS), enabling data-driven budget allocation and campaign optimization.

The dashboard provides a complete view of marketing effectiveness by tracking revenue, advertising spend, conversions, CPA, CPM, CTR, and engagement metrics while uncovering trends and growth opportunities.

**Features / Highlights**
**1. Business Problem**

Marketing teams often struggle to determine:

Which campaigns generate the highest revenue.
Which marketing channels deliver the best ROI.
Whether advertising spend is being allocated efficiently.
Which audience segments respond best to campaigns.
How revenue is trending over time.
Which campaigns should be scaled, optimized, or discontinued.

Without a centralized analytics solution, decision-making becomes reactive rather than data driven.

**2. Goal of the Dashboard**

The primary objectives of this dashboard are to:

✅ Monitor overall campaign performance

✅ Measure marketing efficiency using ROAS, CPA, and CPM

✅ Compare channel performance across App, Email, SMS, and Social Media

✅ Analyze audience performance and revenue contribution

✅ Identify top-performing campaigns

✅ Track revenue growth trends over time

✅ Support marketing budget optimization decisions

✅ Enable executives to quickly evaluate campaign profitability

4. Business Impact & Insights
Revenue Performance
Generated $32.19M total revenue from approximately $16.32M ad spend, resulting in an overall ROAS of 1.97.
Every marketing dollar invested generated nearly $1.97 in return revenue.
Campaign Optimization

Identified high-performing campaigns including:

Referral Program
New User Discount
Email Campaign

Enabled marketers to prioritize campaigns delivering superior ROI.

Channel Effectiveness

**Analysis reveals:**

Email channel delivered the highest ROAS (2.05).
SMS campaigns maintained strong profitability.
Social Media generated the largest revenue contribution.
App campaigns remained effective but showed optimization opportunities.
Audience Insights

**Audience analysis helped determine:**

New Users generated the largest revenue contribution.
Premium users achieved strong engagement rates.
Audience-specific optimization opportunities exist for acquisition campaigns.
Trend Monitoring
MoM Revenue Growth tracking highlights periods of strong and weak performance.
Rolling averages smooth short-term fluctuations and help identify long-term trends.
Budget Allocation Benefits

**The dashboard supports:**

Better budget distribution across channels.
Improved campaign scaling decisions.
Reduced spending on underperforming campaigns.
Increased focus on high-return audience segments.
